Cartoon NPC GLB asset set
Each character is an independent GLB with named runtime parts and animated joint anchors.
Humanoids share joint names: root_motion, hips, spine, head_joint, shoulder/elbow/hand L/R, knee/foot L/R.
Quadrupeds use: root_motion, body_joint, head_joint, tail_joint, front/back paw L/R.
Every file includes a 48-frame walk_cycle-style animation and anchors for navigation/look targeting.
