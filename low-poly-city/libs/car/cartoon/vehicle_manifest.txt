Cartoon vehicle GLB asset set
Units: meters. Blender forward axis exports as glTF/Three.js friendly +Z up.
Every vehicle is an independent GLB file.
Objects named *_window_*, *_wheel_*, *_light_* and police_siren_* are separated for runtime control.
Custom glTF extras: threejs_role, destructible, hit_points, optional spin_axis/detachable.

Files:
- cartoon_small_car.glb
- cartoon_police_car.glb
- cartoon_truck.glb
