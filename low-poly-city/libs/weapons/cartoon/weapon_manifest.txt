Cartoon first-person weapon GLB asset set
Purpose: stylized Three.js runtime assets, not mechanical reference models.
Every weapon is an independent GLB file.
Runtime parts are separated and named: scope, lens, magazine, bullets, trigger, muzzle, flash socket, rocket, pull ring.
glTF extras include threejs_role, runtime_part, detachable, ammo_related, and visible_by_default where useful.

Files:
- cartoon_sniper_rifle.glb
- cartoon_ak47.glb
- cartoon_m14.glb
- cartoon_grenade.glb
- cartoon_rocket_launcher.glb
