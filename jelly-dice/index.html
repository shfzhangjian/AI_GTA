Made to be touched.

Tap a die for a little squish. Pull gently to stretch it; keep pulling to lift. Drag the empty table to orbit. Scroll or pinch to get closer.

Space / R rolls the dice. Esc closes settings. Tab to the playground, then 1–5 selects a die, Enter pokes it, and the arrow keys give it a small nudge. All settings work with Tab and the arrow keys.

Motion is calmer when your device requests reduced motion. Sound starts off.
